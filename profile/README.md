# Cloud Community Labs

**The engine behind community-run cloud conferences.**

Cloud Community Labs is a 501(c)(3) non-profit founded by organizers, for organizers. We give community-run cloud conferences a legal and financial home, so the people building them can focus on the program, the speakers and the community.

You bring the community and the content. We become your legal and financial home.

[Visit cloudcommunitylabs.io](https://cloudcommunitylabs.io/)

## What we do

We provide fiscal sponsorship and operational support for community-driven events in the cloud native ecosystem. For a typical community conference, we handle:

- The legal entity and bank account
- Venue and vendor contracts
- Sponsor invoicing and collection
- Event insurance
- Ticketing payouts and refunds
- Tax filings and compliance

Organizers keep the program, the speakers, the community and the day itself.

[How fiscal sponsorship works](https://cloudcommunitylabs.io/what-we-do/)
